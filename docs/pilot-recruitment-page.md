# University School AI — Pilot Program Recruitment

## 🎓 Start College at 14.
### Free 3-Week Pilot for Homeschool Students Ages 13–17

---

### What Is This?

University School AI is building the future of education — and we need your teenager to help us prove it works.

We've created **interactive learning missions** that teach university-level courses (Physics, Economics, Biology, Psychology) through **choose-your-own-adventure stories** powered by AI. No lectures. No worksheets. Just immersive stories where your student makes decisions, faces consequences, and discovers concepts through experience.

**We're looking for 15 homeschool students to play through 4 missions over 3 weeks — completely free.**

---

### What Your Student Will Experience

| Mission | Subject | What Happens |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| 🛩️ **The Wright Problem** | Physics | Travel to Kitty Hawk, 1903. Help the Wright Brothers solve flight using Newton's Laws. |
| 👟 **The Drop** | Economics | Launch a limited-edition sneaker. Learn supply, demand, and equilibrium through hype culture. |
| 🧬 **Respawn** | Biology | Die in a video game, wake up inside your own cells. Navigate mitosis. Skip a checkpoint? Become cancer. |
| 📱 **The Algorithm** | Psychology | Design a social media app. Discover you're engineering Pavlov's conditioning — on yourself. |

**Time commitment**: ~45 minutes per mission, 2 missions per week = **~2 hours/week for 3 weeks**

### Who Should Apply?

- **Ages 13–17**
- **Homeschooled or micro-schooled** (FL or AZ preferred, but all states welcome)
- **No subject prerequisites** — the missions are designed for zero prior knowledge
- **Comfortable with a computer/tablet** and basic web navigation
- **Curious** — that's the only requirement that actually matters

---

### What This Is NOT

❌ This is not an online class — there's no teacher, no schedule, no homework  
❌ This is not a test — there are no "right answers." Every path teaches.  
❌ This is not screen-time — each mission is designed to be 30-40 minutes, then stop  
❌ This is not data harvesting — all student data stays on their device. We collect only anonymized, aggregate learning outcomes.

---

### How It Works

1. **Fill out the interest form** (2 minutes) → [Link]
2. **We'll send a consent form** via DocuSign
3. **Your student creates an account** and takes a quick 20-question pre-assessment
4. **15-minute orientation Zoom** (parent + student) — we show them one mission scene
5. **Weeks 1-2**: Play 4 missions at their own pace
6. **Week 3**: Post-assessment + group discussion + replay favorite mission
7. **Week 5**: Quick follow-up assessment (checks retention)

**Total parent time: ~30 minutes** (consent + orientation). Everything else is self-directed.
