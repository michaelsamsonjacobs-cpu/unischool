### Purpose

Your child is invited to participate in a pilot program testing interactive educational software developed by University School AI. The purpose is to evaluate whether AI-generated branching narrative missions can improve understanding of university-level concepts in Physics, Economics, Biology, and Psychology.

### What Your Child Will Do

1. Complete a **20-question pre-assessment** (10 minutes) to establish a baseline
2. Play through **4 interactive learning missions** over 2 weeks (~30-45 minutes each)
3. Write **4 brief reflection entries** (1 per mission, ~5-10 minutes each)
4. Complete **weekly engagement surveys** (2-3 minutes each)
5. Complete a **20-question post-assessment** (10 minutes)
6. Optionally participate in a **15-minute interview** (via Zoom)
7. Complete a **retention assessment** 2 weeks after the post-test (10 minutes)

**Total estimated time commitment: 5-6 hours over 4 weeks**

### Data Collection

The following data will be collected:

**From your child:**
- Assessment scores (pre-test, post-test, retention test)
- Mission completion data (decisions made, paths taken, time spent)
- Reflection journal entries
- Weekly survey responses
- Interview responses (if participating)

**Technical data collected automatically by the software:**
- Which narrative nodes were visited
- Which choices were made at decision points
- Time spent per scene
- XP (experience points) earned
- Misconception branches explored

### Data Privacy & Security

- **All student data is stored locally** on your child's device (not transmitted to the cloud)
- Anonymized aggregate data may be used in research reports, grant applications, and product improvement
- **No personally identifiable information (PII)** will be included in any published materials
- Your child's individual results will be shared only with you (the parent/guardian)
- Data handling complies with **COPPA** and **FERPA** regulations

### Risks

- **Minimal risk** — equivalent to normal educational computer use
- Some mission content involves age-appropriate decision-making scenarios
- Screen time is limited to 45 minutes per session
- If any content causes concern, you may withdraw your child immediately

### Benefits

- **Free access** to 4 university-level learning missions
- **Pre/post learning assessment report** shared with you
- **$25 gift card** for your child upon completion of all 4 missions
- **$10 gift card** for you as a thank-you for participation
- **Early access** to future missions before public launch

### Voluntary Participation

Participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw your child at any time, for any reason, without penalty. Withdrawal will not affect any future relationship with University School AI.

Contact us at pilot@universityschool.ai or call [phone number] with any questions.
