## The Big One: Federal Charter Schools Program (CSP)

The U.S. Department of Education's **Charter Schools Program** is the largest single source of startup funding for new schools. In 2025, Congress appropriated **$500 million** for this program.

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| **What it funds** | Startup & first-year operating costs for new charter/micro-schools |
| **How much** | **$150,000 – $500,000+** per school (varies by state) |
| **Who applies** | You do, through your state's education agency |
| **Timeline** | Typically 12-18 months from application to funding |
| **What it covers** | Facility costs, equipment, curriculum/technology, staff training, marketing, initial operations |

## State-by-State Funding Opportunities

### 🌴 Florida
**Why FL is great for franchisees:** One of the most charter-friendly states. Strong school choice culture, ESA program, and large demand for alternative education options.

| Program | Amount | Details |
|---------|--------|---------|
| **FL Charter School Program (CSP)** | $150-400K | Startup subgrants through FLDOE for new charter schools |
| **Schools of Hope** | Up to $500K | For schools serving "persistently low-performing" neighborhoods |
| **Family Empowerment Scholarship** | ~$8,700/student/yr | State-funded scholarship that follows the student — parents choose your school |
| **Gardiner Scholarship (Step Up)** | ~$10,000/student/yr | For students with disabilities — fully portable |

**Franchise Owner Action:** Register as a FL public charter school with your local district or authorizer. Once chartered, apply for CSP subgrants through FLDOE.

### 🌞 California
**Why CA is great for franchisees:** Largest K-12 market in the US. Strong tech culture means parents actively seek innovative education models.

| Program | Amount | Details |
|---------|--------|---------|
| **CA CSP Subgrants** | $150-500K | Through CDE for new or expanding charter schools |
| **CA Education Technology Grants** | $50-200K | CDE innovation and digital learning grants |
| **CA Arts Council (Arts Education)** | $10-50K | For integrating arts into STEM curriculum |
| **Prop 28 Arts Funding** | Varies | Guaranteed arts education funding for all CA public schools |

**Franchise Owner Action:** Apply to an authorizer (local district or county office). CA charter law is evolving — contact the California Charter Schools Association (CCSA) for current guidance.

## Your Funding Playbook (Step-by-Step)

### Phase 1: Choose Your State (Month 1)
1. Review this guide and pick the state where you want to open
2. Contact the state's charter school association for current requirements
3. Identify your authorizer (who will approve your charter application)

### Phase 2: Charter Application (Months 2-4)
1. University School AI provides a **turnkey charter application template** customized for your state
2. The application leverages our evidence-based pedagogy, AI technology, and Berkeley affiliation
3. Submit application to your authorizer
4. Typical review: 60-120 days

### Phase 3: Grant Applications (Months 3-6)
1. Once your charter is approved (or concurrently), apply for **CSP subgrants** through your state
2. University School AI's A-DIGER system **automatically identifies** additional federal and state grants you're eligible for
3. Our AI-assisted grant writer generates first-draft proposals tailored to each opportunity
4. Apply to multiple programs simultaneously — they stack

### Phase 4: Launch (Months 6-12)
1. Use CSP funds for facility, equipment, and technology setup
2. University School AI franchise license includes the full XP Engine platform, training, and ongoing support
3. Student recruitment using CSP-funded marketing
4. Open your doors
