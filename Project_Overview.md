# University School (Unischool) / Springroll
## Project Overview

### 1. The Vision
**University School** is a next-generation, gamified educational platform that transforms the learning experience into an epic journey. By combining high-fidelity gamification with **Sovereign AI** technology, we empower students to take command of their education in a private, high-performance environment.

### 2. Core Experience: "The Cockpit"
Every student has their own **Cockpit**—a personalized mission control center where they manage their academic career.
- **The Navigator:** A private, local AI mentor that understands the student's unique learning style without compromising data privacy.
- **The Quest Log:** Instead of "assignments," students complete "Quests" and "Missions" that feel like leveling up in a game.
- **The Skill Tree:** A visual map of knowledge. As students learn, their skill tree branches out, showing clear progress and unlocking new specialized paths.

### 3. Sovereign AI Philosophy
Unlike traditional educational tools that rely on cloud-based AI, Unischool pioneers **Local-First Inference**.
- **Privacy:** Student data never leaves their device. Interaction with the AI is 100% private.
- **Performance:** Leveraging the power of local GPUs (via WebGPU and MLC AI), the AI is fast and available even offline.
- **Ownership:** Students own their learning data, insights, and digital portfolio.

### 4. Ecosystem Roles
- **Students:** The pilots. Navigating their education through quests and skill trees.
- **Parents:** The mission controllers. Monitoring progress, providing support, and celebrating achievements through the Parent Dashboard.
- **Advisors/Facilitators:** The mentors. Guiding students, providing human insight, and managing the classroom environment.
- **Franchise Owners:** The entrepreneurs. Launching local "Springroll Team" hubs to scale the University School model to their communities.
- **Super Admins:** The architects. Managing the global infrastructure and platform growth.

### 5. Design Aesthetic: "Playful Yet Academic"
The visual language of Unischool is **Solarpunk meets High-Performance Academy**. 
- **Colors:** Crimson (#8B2332) for academic excellence, Gold (#C9B47C) for achievement, and Midnight (#0A1628) for the tech-forward dashboard.
- **Typography:** Playfair Display for headers (Academic) and Inter for UI (Clean/Modern).
- **Vibe:** It feels like a premium pilot's dashboard—refined, tactical, and deeply rewarding.

### 6. Key Technological Pillars
- **Local LLMs:** Integration with WebLLM and Transformers.js for offline-capable, private intelligence.
- **Cross-Platform:** Built with Tauri for a seamless desktop experience (Windows/Mac/Linux) and Vite for a high-performance web dashboard.
- **Cloud Synchronization:** Supabase provides secure auth and database synchronization for multi-device support.
- **Monetization:** Stripe-powered subscription tiers (Pro, Team, Enterprise) for sustainable growth.
