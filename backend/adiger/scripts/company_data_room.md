## 2. Product: XP Engine by University School

University School's XP Engine is an **AI-powered adaptive learning platform** that transforms university lecture content into interactive, branching-narrative RPG experiences. It is designed to make remote college coursework engaging, memorable, and measurably effective for K-12 students pursuing dual enrollment or early college credit.

### Core Architecture (3-Layer Intelligence Stack)
1. **Layer 1 — Multimodal Content Ingestion:** Processes lecture videos, syllabi, PDFs, and course materials using Gemma 4 26B (local multimodal AI). Extracts transcripts, concept graphs, and misconception maps in a single inference pass. No data leaves the student's machine — full sovereignty.
2. **Layer 2 — Concept Graph & Narrative Engine:** Transforms extracted concepts into branching-choice narrative trees using Productive Failure pedagogy. "Wrong" answers are not dead ends but memorable correction scenes backed by research showing 20-40% retention improvement.
3. **Layer 3 — Adaptive Assessment & Learning Analytics:** Tracks concept mastery, misconception exploration, time-on-task, and decision patterns. Generates per-student learning profiles for advisors and franchise operators.

## 3. Pedagogical Foundation

University School's approach is grounded in three evidence-based frameworks:

1. **Productive Failure (Kapur, 2008-2016):** Students who struggle through incorrect problem-solving approaches before receiving instruction outperform those who receive direct instruction first. Our branching narratives deliberately include "wrong" paths that provide memorable correction experiences.

2. **Spaced Retrieval Practice (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006):** The XP Engine spaces concept revisitation across narrative nodes, ensuring students encounter key ideas multiple times in different contexts.

3. **Narrative Transport Theory (Green & Brock, 2000):** Embedding academic concepts within compelling historical narratives increases engagement and reduces cognitive resistance to difficult material.
